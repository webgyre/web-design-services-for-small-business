# Local SEO Guide for Small Business

Local SEO helps businesses become more discoverable when people search for relevant products or services in a specific area.

## Start with accurate business information

Keep the business name, address, phone number, hours, and website consistent wherever the business is listed.

## Create useful service pages

Explain:

- What you offer
- Who it is for
- Where you serve
- What makes the service useful
- How customers can contact you

Do not create thin pages simply by swapping city names.

## Build a strong Google Business Profile

Keep the profile accurate and complete. Add relevant services, photos, business information, and updates where appropriate.

## Earn genuine reviews

Ask real customers for honest reviews. Do not buy reviews or create fake reviews.

## Build local relevance

Useful signals can include:

- Relevant local partnerships
- Community involvement
- Local publications
- Industry directories
- Helpful location content

Focus on genuine relationships rather than mass directory submissions.

## Make the website locally useful

Where relevant, include:

- Service area
- Address
- Contact information
- Directions
- Local service details
- Local testimonials

## Measure performance

Track:

- Organic search traffic
- Local search visibility
- Calls
- Directions
- Form submissions
- Qualified enquiries

Local SEO is a long-term process. Prioritize accuracy, usefulness, and trust.
