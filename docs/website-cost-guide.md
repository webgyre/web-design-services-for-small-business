# Website Cost Guide for Small Business

There is no single correct price for a small business website. Cost depends on scope, complexity, content, integrations, design requirements, and ongoing support.

## What affects the cost?

### 1. Number of pages

A five-page brochure website generally requires less work than a large site with dozens of service and location pages.

### 2. Custom design

A custom design process can include:

- Discovery
- Wireframes
- Visual design
- Responsive layouts
- Prototyping
- Design system

### 3. Development

Development cost can change based on:

- CMS requirements
- E-commerce
- Custom functionality
- APIs
- Authentication
- Databases
- Third-party integrations

### 4. Content

Content may include:

- Copywriting
- Photography
- Video
- Illustrations
- Product information

Good content is an important part of a good website.

### 5. SEO and growth

Technical SEO, content strategy, local SEO, analytics, conversion optimization, and ongoing growth work may be separate from initial design and development.

## A practical way to budget

Instead of choosing a website based only on page count, define:

1. Business goal
2. Target audience
3. Required pages
4. Required features
5. Content responsibility
6. Integrations
7. SEO requirements
8. Launch deadline
9. Ongoing maintenance

Then request a scope-based proposal.

## Avoid comparing agencies only by price

A cheaper website is not necessarily cheaper for the business if it produces poor usability, slow performance, weak SEO, or low conversion.

Compare:

- Scope
- Design process
- Development quality
- Performance
- Accessibility
- SEO foundations
- Support
- Ownership
- Timeline

For an exact project estimate, define the requirements first.
