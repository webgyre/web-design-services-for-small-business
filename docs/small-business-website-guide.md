# Small Business Website Guide

A successful small business website should do more than look professional. It should make the business easy to understand, easy to trust, and easy to contact.

## 1. Define the goal

Start with one primary business goal.

Examples:

- Generate enquiries
- Receive bookings
- Sell products
- Generate phone calls
- Build local visibility
- Explain professional services

A website can support several goals, but one primary conversion should be obvious.

## 2. Know the audience

Identify:

- Who your customers are
- What problem they need solved
- What questions they have
- What makes them trust a provider
- What action you want them to take

Write website copy around customer problems rather than only describing your company.

## 3. Essential pages

Most small businesses should consider:

- Home
- About
- Services
- Individual service pages where useful
- Portfolio or Work
- Testimonials or Reviews
- FAQ
- Contact
- Privacy Policy
- Terms where appropriate

Local businesses may also benefit from location-specific pages when they genuinely serve those areas.

## 4. Design for mobile first

A large percentage of visitors may arrive from mobile devices. Make sure:

- Text is readable
- Buttons are easy to tap
- Navigation is simple
- Images load efficiently
- Forms are short and usable
- Important information appears early

## 5. Build trust

Useful trust signals include:

- Clear business information
- Real project examples
- Customer reviews
- Professional photography
- Transparent service information
- Contact details
- Relevant certifications or memberships
- Secure HTTPS

Never publish invented testimonials, client logos, awards, or performance claims.

## 6. Make the call to action obvious

Use clear actions such as:

- Request a Quote
- Book a Call
- Get Started
- Request a Consultation
- Call Us

Avoid making visitors search for the next step.

## 7. Think about SEO from the beginning

Use:

- One clear page topic
- Descriptive page titles
- Useful headings
- Descriptive URLs
- Helpful internal links
- Image alt text
- Fast performance
- Mobile-friendly layouts
- Relevant, original content

SEO should support users first.

## 8. Measure results

After launch, track meaningful outcomes such as:

- Form submissions
- Calls
- Bookings
- Purchases
- Search visibility
- Conversion rate
- Core performance metrics

A website is an ongoing business asset, not a one-time brochure.
